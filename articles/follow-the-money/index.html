Where Emmer's joint fundraising money goes

Most of the money raised in Emmer's name doesn't go to Emmer's campaign

Emmer Majority Builders is a joint fundraising committee: a donor writes one large check, and it is split among several committees, each within its own legal limit. Chapin has no committee like it. Click the largest band to see the campaigns it funds.

The biggest checks

Individuals who gave $100,000 or more through Emmer's committees this cycle, combined across his campaign, his leadership PAC and his two joint fundraising committees. Most of each check passes through to the party and other candidates; no one can give Emmer's own campaign more than $7,000 for the primary and general combined. No one gave Chapin $100,000. His largest donors gave $7,000, the legal maximum.

What the record shows. For the largest donors and every Minnesota donor on the list, we checked what they do, how much of their federal giving went to Emmer's committees rather than other campaigns, whether their companies lobbied Congress in 2025 or 2026, and whether Emmer acted on anything they lobbied for. "Nothing found" means we searched FEC, lobbying and congress.gov records and found no link, not that none exists. Where we list a donation date next to an Emmer action, it is chronology only; we are not suggesting any donor's money caused any action.

How we did this

  • Totals come from each committee's pre-general report, covering through Oct. 14, 2026. Line items come from the original FEC filings for every report this cycle.
  • For Emmer's campaign, the map and charts include the donors behind the money his joint fundraising committees transferred in, as itemized on his reports. That base is larger than his "individual contributions" line, because those transfers are reported separately. The percentages are the meaningful figures.
  • Contributions of $200 or less aren't itemized, so their geography and jobs aren't known for either campaign.
  • "The biggest checks" adds up each person's contributions across all four of Emmer's committees, including money passed on to other candidates.
  • Spot an error? Write to mn06watch@gmail.com and we'll correct it with the date noted.
