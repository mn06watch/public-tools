How this page works

  • Vote counts and calls: NBC News results pages, including race calls by the NBC News Decision Desk. A race shows as won or lost only after NBC calls it. "Leading" and "Trailing" are uncalled races and can flip as more votes come in. Early and mail ballots often report first.
  • Kalshi: the current price on Kalshi's market for the race, shown as the chance the market gives Emmer's candidate. It's a betting market, not a projection. Some races have no Kalshi market.
  • The races: every Republican on the On the Road log, plus MN-06. The money and Cook ratings come from that log.
  • Updates: a script pulled new numbers from NBC News and Kalshi about every 15 minutes from election night through Nov. 11, 2026, and published them here automatically.
