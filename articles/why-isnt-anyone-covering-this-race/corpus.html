Method and limits

Compiled from indexed news results using the query "Doug Chapin" Emmer Minnesota, retrieved September 14, 2026. Pairing the challenger's name with the incumbent's excludes unrelated coverage and eliminates false matches on common names. This is an index, not a census: paywalled and non-indexed items may be missing, and community-weekly coverage from Adams Publishing Group papers is likely undercounted. "Trigger" records what prompted each item — a calendar event such as an endorsement or filing deadline, an event the challenger organized, the congressman's absence from an event, or a columnist's roundup. Not one item in this list was prompted by the two candidates appearing together. If you know of an item missing from this list, we will add it and say so.
