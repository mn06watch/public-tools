Tom Emmer

Republican · Incumbent · Delano

Has represented the 6th District since 2015. House Majority Whip, the No. 3 Republican in the House. Former state legislator and 2010 Republican nominee for governor; attorney.

Doug Chapin

DFL · Challenger · Hanover

Election-law expert. Led The Pew Charitable Trusts' elections team, founded electionline.org and built the University of Minnesota's certificate program in election administration.

Photos: Emmer, official congressional portrait; Chapin, provided by the campaign.

The race: Cook Political Report rates it Solid Republican, a rating it last set on Feb. 6, 2025, more than 20 months ago; it hasn't updated the race since (checked Oct. 28). The district leans R+10. The only public poll is Chapin's own campaign poll, released in August, which showed Emmer ahead by 10. Among voters who already knew who Chapin was, it was tied; that group is small and self-selected, so it doesn't predict the result. In 2024, Emmer won by 25.

Emmer's record over time

House Republicans moved right, and Emmer moved further

Voteview score for each Congress he has served (red), against the median House Republican in the same Congress (dashed). Voteview is an academic measure built from every roll-call vote; higher is more conservative.

EmmerMedian House Republican

Both lines rose: the median House Republican went from +0.490 in 2015–16 to +0.544 now. Through 2021–22, Emmer voted to the left of the typical House Republican. In 2023–24 and 2025–26, the years he has been Majority Whip, he voted to the right of it. His career score, +0.468, averages all six Congresses, which is why it reads as moderate.

Who's paying

Most of Emmer's money comes from outside Minnesota; most of Chapin's comes from inside it

From every FEC filing through Oct. 14, 2026.

The money, by the numbers

  • Raised: Emmer for Congress $10.86 million, about half of it transferred in from his joint fundraising committees. Chapin $765,076.
  • Cash on hand, Oct. 14: Emmer $2.97 million; Chapin $118,476.
  • From inside the district: 2% to 3% of Emmer's itemized individual money; about half of Chapin's.
  • PACs: $2.52 million to Emmer, led by banking, finance and insurance; $17,734 to Chapin, mostly labor and small political committees.

What the record shows

  • Emmer's largest joint committee has passed along $19.3 million; 81% went to the NRCC and other Republicans' races.
  • 67 people gave $100,000 or more through his committees. For most, we found nothing tying their giving to an Emmer action. For Cambria's owners, we laid out the dates next to a bill he cosponsored.
  • A crypto-funded super PAC sent $152,683 in mail for Emmer; Indivisible paid $4,635 for billboards for Chapin.
  • Chapin's campaign has paid a firm he co-owns for his partner's consulting work, at what he says is the rate she quotes other clients.

Showing up
