How we built this

  • Scope. General-election debates, forums and airtime offers for every U.S. House, Senate and governor race on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot, through Oct. 16. Primary debates are excluded. In top-two states, the two finalists are the pair even when they share a party.
  • One candidate showed up means a host invited both major candidates, one declined or didn't respond, and the host still gave the time to the one who came: an empty-podium debate, an interview in the debate slot, or a town hall offered to both. "Didn't appear" includes not responding; the source notes say when a refusal was explicit.
  • Evidence. Verified means a host, broadcaster or news outlet reported it. Claimed means only a campaign, party or partisan outlet did. Unconfirmed means we couldn't recheck the source. Counts at the top use verified events only.
  • No record found means we checked district debate listings, public broadcasters, League of Women Voters forums and statewide roundups, and found nothing either way. Each such row lists what we searched. Many House districts have no public debate listing at all, so this is not a finding that no debate exists.
  • Ratings and polls in the race details come from Sabato's Crystal Ball and nonpartisan public polls, as of the date a candidate declined.
  • Corrections. This census may not be complete. If we missed a debate, an invitation or an empty podium, email mn06watch@gmail.com and we'll add it with the date noted.
