Every correction and update to the Who Showed Up census and series is listed here, newest first. Small fixes to layout and links aren't listed. Changes to a fact, a count or a race are.

  • Oct. 20, 2026
    Tracker: Minnesota cards

    The Minnesota 5th District card left out DeVelle L. Jackson and listed the Oct. 13 NAACP forum without saying it is claimed and not yet verified. The card now shows all three candidates, who appeared, and the claimed label. The race table already showed both.

    Caught by MN-06 Watch.

  • Oct. 19, 2026
    Tracker: Minnesota 5th District

    Added Independent candidate DeVelle L. Jackson, who is on the Nov. 3 ballot and was missing from the race listing. Added an Oct. 13 Minneapolis NAACP Political Action Committee candidate forum. Jackson says he attended and that Rep. Ilhan Omar and John Nagel did not. The forum is labeled Claimed until the host confirms who was invited and who attended.

    Reported by the Jackson campaign. Headline counts unchanged.

  • Oct. 17, 2026
    Part 1, "Hold the Podium"

    Corrected the days until the election from 18 to 17. The page first went up with the count from the day it was built, not the day it was published.

    Caught by MN-06 Watch.

  • Oct. 16, 2026
    Tracker

    Published the census: 506 House, Senate and governor races, every event we could verify where a host invited both candidates and one declined, and links to watch the debates.
